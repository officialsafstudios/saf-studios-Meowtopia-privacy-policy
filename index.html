How Meowtopia handles your data

01

Introduction

SAF Studios (“we,” “our,” or “us”) developed Meowtopia: Kitty Sudoku for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the game and contact us through this website.

The current version of the game does not require an account, player profile, payment details, or cloud backup. Most gameplay information is generated and stored locally on your device.

This policy distinguishes information handled by SAF Studios from information handled by third-party services. The most significant third-party service used by the game is Google Mobile Ads / AdMob.

This policy applies to Meowtopia: Kitty Sudoku and this SAF Studios privacy page. It does not cover third-party advertising landing pages, other apps, or websites that you may open from an advertisement.

02

Information We Collect

Information you choose to provide.

  • If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and message contents you choose to send.
  • The game does not ask you to create an account or provide your name, contacts, photos, microphone input, camera input, or precise location.

Gameplay information generated on your device.

  • The game stores puzzle progress, completed levels, star ratings, settings, and in-progress board state locally.
  • SAF Studios does not operate a server that receives your saved boards or gameplay progress.

Advertising and device information.

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app and device information, advertising interactions, and diagnostic data as described in the Advertising and Google AdMob section.
  • That advertising data is handled by Google and its advertising partners under their own policies, not as SAF Studios account data.
03

Information Stored on Your Device

The game uses Android SharedPreferences to store the following information locally on your device. These records are not uploaded to SAF Studios.

Stored information Purpose
whisker_grid_active_level Remembers which level you were playing.
whisker_grid_save_lvl_<level> Saves an unfinished board, including level number, grid size, placed cats, X marks, hearts, timestamp, and rewarded-ad usage flags.
whisker_grid_reward_life_lvl_<level> and whisker_grid_reward_retry_lvl_<level> Tracks whether a rewarded Extra Life or Extra Retry was already used for a level.
whisker_grid_completed_levels and whisker_grid_stars Stores completed levels and earned star ratings.
whisker_grid_sound_enabled, whisker_grid_music_enabled, and whisker_grid_haptics_enabled Stores your sound, music, and haptic feedback preferences.

These storage keys are implementation details and may change in future app versions. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes this local information.

04

Advertising and Google AdMob

Meowtopia: Kitty Sudoku displays ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The SDK initializes when the app starts and requests ads over the internet. SAF Studios does not add your name, email address, saved puzzle boards, or star ratings to ad requests.

Banner ads

Adaptive banner ads may appear on the gameplay screen.

Interstitial ads

Full-screen ads may appear after eligible gameplay events such as level completion, replay, next-level navigation, restart, or game-over retry, subject to frequency limits.

Rewarded ads

Rewarded ads appear only when you choose features such as Extra Life, Extra Retry, or Hint.

Depending on your device, region, ad availability, and Google settings, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or process:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from network information.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, and language settings.
  • Advertising identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available and permitted.
  • Ad impressions, clicks, views, interactions, and the advertisements shown to you.
  • Diagnostic, crash, performance, and fraud-prevention information.

Google may use this information to deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, provide analytics, and personalize ads where permitted and enabled. Google may share information with advertisers, ad networks, ad exchanges, demand partners, and measurement providers under its own policies.

You can review or reset advertising preferences through Google Ads Settings and Android advertising controls where available. Ads may also include privacy choices supplied by Google or the advertiser.

The current version of the app does not provide a separate SAF Studios advertising personalization toggle.

05

Third-Party Services

Google Mobile Ads / AdMob. Google provides the advertising SDK used by the game. Google's Privacy Policy and advertising controls apply to data processed by Google's advertising services.

Advertisers and ad partners. Ads may be supplied by Google demand partners, advertisers, ad networks, exchanges, or measurement providers. Those parties may process information under their own privacy policies.

External links. Tapping an ad may open an advertiser's website or another external destination. SAF Studios is not responsible for the data practices of those external services.

Device capability checks. The haptics package may query Android device information to determine whether the device supports vibration. This information is used locally and is not transmitted to SAF Studios.

The current version of the game does not include a SAF Studios analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, social login SDK, cloud-save SDK, or in-app purchase service. The Google Mobile Ads plugin includes WebView components used to render advertising content.

06

How We Use Information

SAF Studios uses information for the following purposes:

  • To save and restore unfinished puzzle boards.
  • To remember completed levels, star ratings, and progression.
  • To remember sound, music, and haptic feedback preferences.
  • To enforce once-per-level rewarded-ad benefits where applicable.
  • To respond to privacy questions and other messages sent to our contact email.

Google and its advertising partners use advertising-related information for the purposes described in the Advertising and Google AdMob section.

07

Data Sharing

SAF Studios does not sell your local gameplay information and does not share saved puzzle boards, level progress, or settings with advertisers through its own code.

If you email us, we may use and retain that correspondence only as needed to respond to you and operate the studio. We may disclose information if required by law or necessary to protect SAF Studios, users, or others.

Google and its advertising partners may share advertising-related information as described in their own policies.

08

Data Security

Local gameplay data is stored through Android's app-private storage mechanisms. Access is controlled by the Android operating system and the security of your device.

Advertising requests and related communications are handled by Google Mobile Ads and Google services under Google's security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and SAF Studios cannot guarantee absolute security.

09

Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until it is overwritten by gameplay, removed by an in-app reset, cleared through Android settings, or deleted when you uninstall the app.

  • Completing a level clears that level's saved board state and active-level marker.
  • Restarting or retrying a level can clear the current saved board state.
  • The Settings option called Reset Progress clears completed-level and star records.
  • Rewarded-ad usage flags may remain for unfinished levels until the level is completed or app data is cleared.

To delete all locally stored game data, open Android Settings, select Apps, choose Meowtopia: Kitty Sudoku, then clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

SAF Studios does not maintain a server-side copy of your gameplay progress, so there is no SAF Studios account data to delete. For advertising data processed by Google, use Google's advertising controls or contact Google under its own privacy policies.

10

Children's Privacy

Meowtopia: Kitty Sudoku does not ask children or adults to provide personal information such as a name, email address, or account credentials.

The game may display ads through Google Mobile Ads. Google's advertising SDK may process device and advertising identifiers as described above. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of apps that display advertising.

If SAF Studios learns that a child has directly provided personal information to us, we will take reasonable steps to delete it. Advertising data processed by Google is handled under Google's policies and controls.

Your Privacy Rights

Because most game data is stored locally, you can delete it through Android's app settings or by uninstalling the game.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that SAF Studios holds, such as information contained in an email you send us. Contact us using the details below and we will respond subject to applicable law.

For advertising identifiers and ad personalization data controlled by Google, use Google Ads Settings, Android advertising controls, or Google's own privacy processes.
